About Brightbyte

A small team that likes clear code and clear explanations.

Our story

Brightbyte started in 2018 with two developers helping local shops get online. Clients kept asking us to teach their staff, so we began running training sessions.

Today we do both: we build software for businesses, and we teach courses for people entering the tech industry.

What we believe

Keep it simple. The best solution is the one your team can understand and maintain.

Teach as we work. Every project ends with documentation your team can use.

Be honest about time and cost. No surprises on the invoice.
